# KaziSafi — Services, Pricing & Marketplace Operations Reference

## 1. Platform Overview & Service Categories
KaziSafi connects Kenyan households with document- and identity-verified service professionals across **8 core categories** (plus specialized Airbnb/Office tiers). All pricing is denominated in **Kenyan Shillings (KES)**.

## 3. High-Trust Add-On Feature: `KeyCollect` (Attended Key Handover)
For customers who cannot remain at home during the service:
* **How it works:** Only admin-elected, top-rated Pros qualify. The customer names an on-site contact (e.g., caretaker, security guard, or neighbor) who receives a time-limited verification link via WhatsApp/SMS showing the Pro's photo, partially masked ID, and live journey tracking.
* **Chain of Custody:** The Pro leaves their **original National ID** with the contact upon collecting the key, completes the job, returns the key, and retrieves their ID.
* **Pricing:** Separate add-on fee of **40% of the booking amount (minimum KES 500)** paid upfront.

## 4. Payment, Escrow & Cancellation Rules
* **Payment Methods:** M-Pesa STK Push (primary), Card, Bank Transfer.
* **Booking Deposit / Escrow Model:** Customers pay either a **50% deposit** (balance on completion) or **100% into M-Pesa Escrow** (released to the Pro's M-Pesa wallet once the customer marks the job complete).
* **Platform Commission:** KaziSafi retains a **15%–20% service commission** per completed booking.
* **Cancellation Policy:**
  * **24+ hours before service:** 100% full refund.
  * **12–24 hours before service:** 50% refund.
  * **Less than 12 hours before service:** No refund.
  * **Service Pro No-Show:** 100% full refund or free priority rescheduling.

## 6. Service Pro Vetting Requirements
1. Valid Kenyan National ID or Passport (front & back upload).
2. DCI Certificate of Good Conduct.
3. Minimum age 18+ with smartphone & internet access.
4. Verifiable references / guarantors (plus trade certificates for Electricians & Plumbers).
